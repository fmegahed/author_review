Why your review matters:

Our research team uses AI (large language models) to automatically extract structured factsheets from arXiv papers. Your expert review helps us assess the quality of these extractions. Results will be reported in aggregate in a manuscript that will be submitted by our team to Quality Engineering. No individual responses will be attributed to you by name. Your feedback will also help us improve the extraction pipeline for future use.

How it works:

For each paper, you'll review AI-extracted information and rate each field on a 1–5 scale. Most fields are rated for correctness, while the Unstated Future Work field is rated for reasonableness. You can optionally add comments explaining your rating. During the review, hover over any scale label or numbered button to see its full definition.

Correctness scale (used for most fields)
ScoreAnchorDefinition
1IncorrectThe response is wrong, unsupported by the paper, or clearly misrepresents the paper’s content.
2Mostly IncorrectThe response contains a small amount of correct information, but substantial errors, misclassifications, or important omissions remain.
3Partially CorrectThe response is a mix of correct and incorrect information, or it captures only part of what is supported by the paper.
4Mostly CorrectThe response is largely correct and supported by the paper, with only minor errors, omissions, or imprecision.
5CorrectThe response is correct, supported by the paper, and does not contain meaningful errors or omissions.

Reasonableness scale (used for Unstated Future Work)
ScoreAnchorDefinition
1UnreasonableThe suggested future work is implausible, disconnected from the paper, or not meaningfully motivated by its content.
2Mostly UnreasonableThe suggestion has limited connection to the paper and is only weakly justified by its methods, findings, or discussion.
3Somewhat ReasonableThe suggestion is partially plausible and somewhat related to the paper, but the justification is incomplete or only moderately convincing.
4Mostly ReasonableThe suggestion is plausible, relevant to the paper, and reasonably supported by its content, with only minor weaknesses in justification.
5ReasonableThe suggested future work is plausible, well aligned with the paper, and strongly justified by its content.
